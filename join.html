How the beta works, in plain words

  • Free for 30 days. You don't pay anything during the beta. If you want to keep Glim afterwards, you choose to subscribe; nothing is charged without you choosing it.
  • Nothing to download. Glim is a web app. Tap the button, sign up, then add it to your home screen from your phone's browser. It opens full-screen like any app.
  • One setup step. You forward unanswered calls to your Glim number with a short code from your carrier. The app shows the code for your carrier, and we help by email if it fights you.
  • Your number stays. Customers keep calling the number they already have.

What we'll ask you to do

  • Use it on your real business phone for the 30 days, with real missed calls.
  • Tell us honestly what happened. What broke, what confused you, what you'd pay for and what you wouldn't. A quick email or a reply to ours is plenty. The things that annoy you are the most useful.
  • Expect rough edges. It's a beta: you may hit a bug, and things will change as we fix them. When something changes, we tell you.
