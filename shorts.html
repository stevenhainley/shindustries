How it works

You send one link

Your latest YouTube episode or audio + video file. Nothing to fill in.

We pick and cut the moments

We pick moments by what makes a stranger stop scrolling in the first two seconds, not by keyword density. Captions are styled for your show, audio is levelled, and each file fits platform upload limits.

You review in a shared folder

Every clip lands in a private Drive folder with a notes file: the hook, the timestamps and a suggested post caption for each. Reply with changes, or just post. The clips are yours.

What you get

  • 8 vertical clips (15–60 s) from one episode, ready for Shorts, Reels and TikTok
  • Captions burned in, styled for your show, with no auto-caption errors left in
  • A hook line and a post caption for each clip
  • 48-hour turnaround and one round of changes
