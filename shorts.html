How it works

One link in. Finished clips out.

1 · Send

You send one link

Your latest YouTube episode, or an audio and video file. Nothing to fill in.

2 · Pick

We pick the moments

By what makes a stranger stop scrolling in the first two seconds, read from the whole transcript.

3 · Cut

Captions, crop, loudness

Captions styled for your show, audio levelled, and every file sized for the platforms.

4 · Review

You review, then post

A private folder with a notes file. Reply with changes, or just post. The clips are yours.

Story

Most founder shows we checked have episodes doing thousands of views and Shorts doing under a hundred. Every week without clips, the best minute of the episode stays inside a long video most people never open. The moments are already recorded; they need cutting, captioning and a hook, and that is the work you pay for.

What you get
  • 8 vertical clips (15–60 s) from one episode, ready for Shorts, Reels and TikTok
  • Captions burned in, styled for your show, with no auto-caption errors left in
  • A hook line and a post caption for each clip
  • Speaker crop for single and two-up layouts
  • 48-hour turnaround and one round of changes
  • A private folder you keep
Refunds
  • Founding pack: not worth $99 to you? Reply within 7 days of delivery for a full refund.
  • Packs: a pack more than 24 hours late is free.
  • Monthly: pause or cancel any time; no refund for a month whose pack was delivered.
