Privacy

Last updated 15 October 2026.

sh. industries is a trade name used by Steven Hainley. This page says what data we handle and why. Contact: stevenhainley@gmail.com.

What this website collects

Nothing about you directly. There are no analytics, no ads, no tracking pixels and no cookies. The site keeps one setting (Simple mode on or off) in your browser's local storage; it never leaves your device.

Services the site loads

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may log IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces, so your browser requests them from Google.

When you buy

Stripe and Gumroad process payments and send us your name, email and what you bought. We use that to deliver the order and answer questions.

When you email us

We keep the thread to do the work and reply. Episodes you send and the clips we make are stored in a private Google Drive folder shared with you.

Release notes

If you ask for release notes, we keep your email address on a list used only for those emails. Ask to be removed at any time.

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent personal data, and we don't use it to train anything.

Your rights

Email us to see, correct or delete what we hold about you. We answer within one business day.
