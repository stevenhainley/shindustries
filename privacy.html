PRIVACY POLICY

Your data stays on your device.

Effective: November 19, 2026 · sh. industries (Steven Hainley, Sole Proprietor, Massachusetts, USA)

1. This website (shindustries.co)

shindustries.co is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses zero advertising cookies, zero tracking pixels, and zero third-party analytics scripts, and bundles its own fonts locally. GitHub Pages may record standard web server request logs (such as IP address and user agent) under GitHub's own privacy policy.

2. Blacktop (Android App & Mobile Web App)

  • Local-first storage: Your shifts, mileage logs, earnings, apartment gate codes, slow-restaurant notes, and money-split settings are stored directly on your device (local Android storage or your browser's local storage). We do not sell, rent, or view your driving data.
  • No third-party account passwords: Blacktop never asks for your DoorDash, Uber, Instacart, Grubhub, Spark, bank, or brokerage passwords.
  • On-device order reading: On Android, order pay and distance are read locally on your phone to calculate pay per mile—nothing is uploaded to ad networks or stored on remote servers.
  • Payments via Stripe: All Pro Pass payments are processed exclusively by Stripe, Inc. (stripe.com/privacy). sh. industries never sees or stores your full card number. We receive only your receipt email, payment confirmation, and license key status so we can unlock Pro on your devices and issue refunds when requested.

3. DuckyGuard (macOS)

DuckyGuard runs 100% locally on your Mac with zero network ports and zero telemetry. It inspects keystroke timing locally inside macOS's CGEvent tap. If an injection burst is blocked, the incident record is written strictly to a local folder on your Mac owned by your user account and is never transmitted anywhere.

4. Archived prototypes (Glim & Mo-ify me)

Glim is currently an archived concept preview and does not collect live customer SMS records or waitlist submissions on this site. Mo-ify me runs face-landmark drawing entirely inside your local browser tab without uploading photos.

5. Email support & data deletion

If you email steven@shindustries.co or purchase a Pro Pass via Stripe, we keep your email solely to send receipts, manage license access, and answer support requests. We never sell or share email addresses. Email us anytime to delete your support or license record.
