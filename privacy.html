Privacy

Last updated: October 26, 2026 (draft)

Glim is made by sh. industries (Steven Hainley, a sole proprietor in Massachusetts). This page says what we collect, why, who sees it, and how to get it deleted. Questions: glim@shindustries.co.

The short version
  • We collect what Glim needs to do its job: your account, your business details, your customer list, and your calls, texts and quotes.
  • We never sell your data or your customers' data.
  • When Glim drafts a message, only the text it needs goes to an AI model.
  • You can export or delete your data any time. Delete your account in the app and it goes.

Who is who

"You" is the business owner who signs up for Glim. "Your customers" are the people who call or text your business. Glim texts your customers on your behalf, so you decide who gets a message. We hold your customers' information for you, to run Glim for you.

What we collect

From you:

  • Your name, email, business name, business type and business phone number.
  • Your Glim settings, such as how you want messages to sound and what needs your OK first.
  • Payment status. Stripe takes your card details. We never see or store your full card number.

About your customers, because Glim needs it to work:

  • Names and phone numbers, and anything you add to their record.
  • Missed-call records (the number that called, and when).
  • The texts Glim sends them and the replies they send back.
  • Quotes and invoices, such as the service or job, the amount and whether it is paid.
  • Whether a customer has replied STOP, so we never text them again.

The founding shops waitlist and beta invites

If you join the waitlist on our site, we keep your email, business name, business type, your answers about missed calls and whether you would pay, anything you write in the optional comment box and, if you give it, your phone carrier. We use them to decide whether to open Glim, to email you once when founding spots open, to send occasional beta updates (each with a one-click unsubscribe) and to help you set up call forwarding. Beta testers who use an invite code also give us the code, so we know who invited them. We never sell this list or share it for marketing. Ask us at glim@shindustries.co and we delete it. What we use it for

  • To text back missed calls, follow up quotes and invoices, and send the messages you approve.
  • To bill you and keep your account running.
  • To answer your emails and fix problems.
  • To keep Glim safe and stop misuse.

We do not use your customers' information to advertise to them, and we do not use it for anything unrelated to running Glim for you.

AI models

Glim uses AI models to draft messages. Today that is Claude, made by Anthropic. When Glim drafts a message, only the text needed for that message is sent, for example the customer's first name, the service or job and the quote amount. We do not send your whole customer list. Your choices

  • Export. You can export your customer list, quotes and message history from the app.
  • Delete. You can delete your customers' records, or your whole account, inside the app. Email us if you are stuck.
  • Customers. If someone texted by your business asks us to see or delete their information, we pass it to the business, and we help the business do it. They can also reply STOP at any time to end texts.

Security

We use reasonable steps to protect your data, including encrypted connections. No system is perfectly safe. If we learn of a problem that affects your data, we will tell you.

Children

Glim is for businesses. It is not meant for anyone under 18.
