How it works, and why your photo stays put

  • A small face model (about 550 KB) finds the nose and lips in your photo. It runs in your browser with face-api.js on TensorFlow.js, loaded from this site.
  • The page's Content Security Policy only lets it talk to its own site, and no code here sends your photo anywhere. No cookies, no analytics, no backend: the host only serves the files.
  • Once the face model has loaded (the page says so under the buttons), it keeps working with the network off.
