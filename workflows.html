What's inside

Three workflows. Each one did real work this week.

Clip engine

Episode in, clip pack out. You pick the moments; it does everything else.

  • Speaker crop for single and two-up layouts
  • Word-highlight captions in your show's colour
  • Hook card, CTA plate, loudness, size limits
  • Notes file with a post caption per clip
  • 48-hour runbook from "yes" to delivered
./sh ingest <url> packs/show · ./sh render packs/show --crop 0.5 --accent "#ff6fae"

Lead finder

Hundreds of podcast feeds in, a short list of shows with a real Shorts gap out, with their public contact email.

  • Feed-first: active shows with a contact address
  • Verified YouTube match, no guessing
  • Shorts views vs episode views, read that day
  • GAP rows are the only ones you pitch
./sh leads "founder podcast" "bootstrapped saas"

Outreach pipeline

One SQLite file and six commands. No CRM, no tabs.

  • add · sent · replied · yes · no · paid
  • Follow-ups due on day 3, with the line to paste
  • The note template that went out 11 times
  • Rule built in: every number verified, none invented
./sh pipeline sent "Startup Dad" · ./sh pipeline followups

Straight answers

Before you pay

Is this the whole system SH runs on?

No. SH also runs a private operations layer (task routing, a morning brief, health checks) that is welded to one Mac and not for sale. The kit is the part that touches customers: finding them, writing to them, delivering clips. It is the part that earned its keep this week.

Does it pick the moments for me?

No, and that is on purpose. The moment choice is the product you sell; the kit removes everything around it. The runbook tells you how to pick in ten minutes.

Will this make me money?

Nobody can promise that and this page won't. What's true: the kit cut the cost of a clip pack to under a dollar of compute and about thirty minutes of your time, and found 23 pitchable shows in an evening. Whether they say yes depends on your notes and your clips.

What do I need?

A Mac, Homebrew, Python 3.9 or newer, about a gigabyte of disk for the speech model, and ten minutes: ./setup.sh installs the tools, fetches the model and self-checks. No accounts, no API keys. Windows and Linux are not supported in v0.1.

Refunds?

Fourteen days, no questions, through the store. Memberships cancel from your account at any time.
