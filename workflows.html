Story

These are the files the studio runs on: the same ones that sent 13 pitches and cut 9 client-ready clips in one evening. The kit is the part that touches customers: finding them, writing to them, delivering clips.

What you need

A Mac, Homebrew, Python 3.9 or newer, about a gigabyte of disk for the speech model, and ten minutes: ./setup.sh installs the tools, fetches the model and checks itself. No accounts, no API keys. Windows and Linux are not supported in v0.1.

Questions
Is this the whole system SH runs on?
No. SH also runs a private operations layer (task routing, a morning brief, health checks) that is tied to one Mac and not for sale. That layer is sh. HQ.
Does it pick the moments for me?
No, on purpose. The moment choice is the product you sell; the kit removes everything around it. The runbook shows how to pick in ten minutes.
Will this make me money?
Nobody can promise that. The kit cut the cost of a clip pack to under a dollar of compute and about thirty minutes of your time; whether shows say yes depends on your notes and your clips.
