Questions

Straight answers.

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can send a text, you can run Glim. Setup is four screens: your business name, your kind of business, how customers reach you, and how much Glim does on its own. The only fiddly bit is forwarding unanswered calls, and that's one code you dial into your phone once. We show it on screen for your carrier.

Stuck? Email glim@shindustries.co and a person answers.

Is this for my kind of business?

If customers call you and you sometimes can't pick up, yes. Glim has ready-made setups for salons and barbers, cleaners, plumbers, HVAC, electricians, landscapers, roofers, painters, handymen, pest control and auto repair. Any other business (a restaurant, a clinic front desk, a gym, a consultant) picks “Something else” and writes its own first question once.

Not a fit: if you need a voice that answers the phone, or you want to send marketing texts to a list. Glim does neither.

What does “beta” mean for me?

Glim works and is being used, but it's new. Some features are still rolling out (see Beta, honestly), you may hit a bug, and we'll ask what you think. You get the founding price, $29 a month for as long as you stay subscribed, and the same 7-day money-back as everyone.

Why can't I buy it yet?

We open sales once a readiness checklist passes: texting registration approved with the carriers, the money-back and cancel flows tested end to end, and support ready. We open the first 10 founding spots when enough businesses say yes, so the waitlist questions are the test: answer them honestly, including “Not for me”. We email you once when the spots open.

Does my phone number change?

No. Customers keep calling the number they already have. Glim gives your business a separate texting number; when a call to your phone rings out, it forwards there and the text-back goes out from it. Every text names your business.

What happens if I go over 400 texts?

We text you at 320 so you see it coming. If you hit 400, Glim keeps answering missed calls and replying to customers who text in, and pauses the optional stuff like quote check-ins. We never add a surprise charge to your bill. If you want more, a pack of 300 texts is $10, and only if you choose to buy one.

Will it text a customer at 2 a.m.?

No. Each kind of business has default quiet hours (most are 8 p.m. to 8 a.m.) and you can change them. A call that comes in overnight gets its text-back in the morning.

Will it send something embarrassing?

Only if you let it. Start on "approve everything" and nothing leaves without your tap. Glim's messages are plain and short, use your business's words, and never promise a price you didn't set. You can edit any draft before it goes.

Is this a real app or a website?

Both, honestly. It's a web app you add to your home screen, so it opens full-screen with its own icon and can notify you. There's no App Store or Google Play listing yet; those come later as updates. You won't pay again for them.

What happens to my customers' data?

Your customer list, quotes and message history are yours. Export or delete them whenever you want. When Glim drafts a reply, only the text it needs goes to an AI model. We don't sell or share anything. The full wording is in the privacy policy, which a lawyer reviews before launch.
