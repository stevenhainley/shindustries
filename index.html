DuckyGuard Free Mac app · 2.1 out now

A person types unevenly. A gadget posing as a keyboard doesn't.

Some USB gadgets pretend to be a keyboard and type a script into your Mac in under a second. DuckyGuard times every key, and when a burst is faster than any hand could type, it blocks the rest before it reaches an app.
