01 // FLAGSHIP RELEASE · UPDATED NOV 25, 2026

DuckyGuard 2.2 for macOS.

Free, MIT-licensed physical endpoint defense against BadUSB and keystroke-injection hardware.

macOS 26 Apple Silicon · v2.2.0 Out Now Free · MIT

DuckyGuard 2.2

Keyboards that type too fast get stopped. Scores inter-key cadence, 1-2 ms key-release signatures, and Spotlight/dropper command sequences inside a CGEvent tap, quarantines untrusted keyboards on plug-in, and pins trusted hardware descriptors with SHA-256.

  • 0 of 124 injected chars landed with Hold on (real Flipper Zero hardware test)
  • New in 2.2: Spotlight opener rule (P1), jittered dropper rule (P2), and indefinite Lockdown mode
  • Tamper-evident hash-chained audit.jsonl + MITRE ATT&CK, YARA, and OCSF exports

Built for both daily Mac use & incident triage

Version 2.2 closes jittered Spotlight-to-Terminal bypasses and adds portable device trust policies and verifiable local forensics.

  • duckyguard lockdown on|off holds every external keyboard when away from your desk
  • duckyguard devices --export / --import moves SHA-256 pinned USB policies between Macs
  • duckyguard audit --verify checks the SHA-256 hash chain across every recorded event
