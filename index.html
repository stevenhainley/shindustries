GoodDucky Free Mac app · coming soon

A person types unevenly. A gadget posing as a keyboard doesn't.

Some USB sticks pretend to be a keyboard and type a script into your Mac in seconds. GoodDucky watches the typing rhythm, and when a burst is too fast and too even to be a person, it pulls focus to its own window so the rest lands there instead.
