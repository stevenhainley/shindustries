FAQ

What buyers ask first.

Do I need an office manager?

No. With one, Glim takes the chasing off their plate so they can run the office. Without one, it covers the phones and the follow-ups, and Full Assistant also answers your overflow line live. Same plans and prices either way. What it doesn't do: the parts of office work that need a person, like ordering parts or walking a client through a site.

Who writes the texts my customers get?

Every message is a written template we set up together, in your company's name, and you see each one before it's switched on. The AI may tidy the wording but can't add or change a number; if it fails, the plain template goes out. It never makes up a price or an amount.

Does it work with Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan or Square?

Not yet. Today Glim works from QuickBooks Online or a spreadsheet import (your office's sheet, Google Sheets or Excel). Use one of the others? Ask me; it isn't connected yet, and I'll tell you straight what works.

What happens if it breaks?

The parts that send texts are plain scheduled code on a cloud server, not my laptop. A failed text retries, and if it still fails the alert comes to me, never to your customer. You can text PAUSE any time. More in What happens if it breaks.

When do I pay, and when does it start?

You pay the setup fee when your first workflow is running on your phone (usually the 7:00 text), not before, and your first month starts that day. Texts to your customers start once the carriers approve business texting, usually 2 to 3 weeks; your own texts with Glim work from day one.

Is there a contract?

No. Flat monthly price, no per-seat fees, cancel any month. Studio Monthly pauses or cancels any time.

Who else uses it?

No shop runs on Glim for the office yet. You'd be one of the first, and I'd set yours up personally. sh. industries itself runs on Glim every day.

What about refunds?

Studio founding pack: a full refund within 7 days of delivery if it isn't worth $99 to you; any pack more than 24 hours late is free. Glim: the setup fee is charged only once your first workflow is running, and you can cancel any month.
