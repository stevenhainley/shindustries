Meet the owner

Story

One person, on purpose.

You deal with the person who cuts your clips. sh. industries is Steven Hainley and Glim, the system he built to handle the slow parts: transcripts, captions, framing, loudness and file sizes. Steven picks every moment and watches every clip before it leaves.

Being small is the point. It keeps prices at a level a weekly show can afford, and it keeps the person who knows your show on every episode.

Three steps, and the first one is refundable.
