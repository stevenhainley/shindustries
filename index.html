03 // BEHIND THE STUDIO

Hi, I'm Steven.

I started sh. industries in August 2025 alongside my Cybersecurity B.S. (Law & Technology minor) at Worcester Polytechnic Institute as a place to ship real, testable software instead of leaving ideas in class folders.

DuckyGuard 2.1 grew out of building a $6 Raspberry Pi Pico BadUSB tool and wanting to engineer the defensive half—hardware-tested against a Flipper Zero with every limitation documented up front.

Blacktop came from driving late-night DoorDash shifts myself and getting tired of paying $100+/year for clunky spreadsheet trackers that ignore deadhead miles, restaurant waits, and 2026 tax rules.
