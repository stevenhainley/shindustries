What Glim does

Glim helps a small business with its office work. It texts back missed calls, follows up quotes and invoices, and drafts messages with the help of AI. You can set Glim to wait for your OK before anything goes out. Glim is a web app you add to your phone's home screen; app-store versions may come later as updates. Features marked "coming soon" are not promised for any date.

We fix reported problems as fast as we can and tell you what changed. Features marked "coming soon" are not promised for any date. People invited with a beta code get Glim free for 30 days in return for honest feedback; nothing is charged unless they choose to subscribe afterwards. Texting your customers: your part

When Glim texts people, it does so as your business, from a Glim number. That means you must follow the law and the carriers' rules. In particular, you agree that:

  • You will only ask Glim to text people who contacted your business or who agreed to hear from it, for example by calling, texting or booking with you.
  • You will not use Glim to send spam, scams, anything illegal, or marketing blasts to people who did not ask. Glim is for service messages such as missed-call replies, quotes, reminders and review requests.
  • You will not tell Glim to text someone who replied STOP. Glim blocks those numbers automatically, and you must not work around that.
  • Your business details are true, including the business name Glim uses for texting registration.

Our full texting terms are on the Text messaging page.

Glim uses AI models, currently Claude by Anthropic, to draft messages. AI can make mistakes. If you turn on sending without your OK, messages go out without you reading them first. You are responsible for the messages sent in your name. We suggest starting with every message waiting for your OK.
