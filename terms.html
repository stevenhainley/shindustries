Who we are

These terms cover anything you buy from sh. industries, a trade name used by Steven Hainley (“we”). Questions go to steven@shindustries.co.

What we sell

  • Glim: an office helper that lives in your texts, in three plans:
    • Office Helper: $750 setup, then $299/month.
    • Office: $1,500 setup, then $499/month.
    • Full Assistant: $2,500 setup, then $799/month.
    Setup is remote or in person. You pay the setup fee when your first workflow is running on your phone (usually the 7:00 text), not before, and your first month starts that day. No contract: cancel any month.
  • sh. Studio: vertical clips cut from an episode you send, delivered to a private folder within 48 hours of your link, with one round of changes.
  • sh. lab Discord tiers: Builder (free), Operator ($19/month), Insider ($49/month), Partner ($149/month). The paid tiers are monthly Stripe subscriptions; each tier's channels are listed in the server's #tiers channel.

Prices and payment

Prices are in US dollars and shown on each product page. Card payments run through Stripe; we never see your full card number. Glim's monthly price is flat, with no per-seat fees. Texting and calls are included up to the plan limits; anything over is billed at our cost, no markup, itemized on the invoice.

You confirm you have the right to have the episode you send clipped. The clips, captions and files we deliver are yours to post, edit and keep. We only show your clips publicly if you agree, which the founding pack asks for in return for its price.

For Glim, you confirm your customers gave you their numbers for the work you do for them. The texts Glim sends are templates you see at setup, in your company's name.

Discord materials

The runbooks, templates and kit in the paid Discord tiers are licensed to you for your own work, including client work. Please don't resell or redistribute them.

Nobody can promise views, leads or revenue, and we don't. The free sample, the refundable founding pack and the free Glim walkthrough exist so you can judge the work first.
