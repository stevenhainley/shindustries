You're the boss

Nothing big goes out without your YES.

Glim asks one question at a time, by text. Answer YES or NO from your phone. Your office manager can answer from theirs too.

  • Approvals: quotes over $5,000 and the firmer invoice reminder at 21 days wait for a YES. After that, the next step is a call from you, never a nasty text.
  • PAUSE: text PAUSE (or STOP) and nothing goes to customers until you text RESUME. You still hear from Glim.
  • Customers who reply STOP are off the list for good, and you're told if they still owe.
  • Hours: a reply to someone who just called goes out between 8 a.m. and 9 p.m., any day. Reminders and quote check-ins go out 9 to 7, Monday to Saturday. A call at 11 at night gets its text at 8 the next morning.

What happens if it breaks

If something fails, I hear about it. Your customers don't.

Everything breaks sometimes. Here's how Glim is built so that, at worst, a text goes out a few minutes late.

Plain code, on a cloud server

The parts that send texts (the schedule, the reminders, STOP handling, quiet hours, approvals) are plain scheduled code on a cloud server, not on my laptop. They keep running when I'm in class or asleep.

The AI only drafts wording

Every message starts as a written template. The AI may tidy the wording but can't add or change a number. If it fails or is slow, the plain template goes out.

Alerts come to me

A failed text retries at 1, 5 and 15 minutes and can't double-text anyone. If it still fails, the alert goes to me, never to your customer, and you never see an error message.

You can stop it any time

Text PAUSE or STOP and nothing goes to customers until you say RESUME.

On timing: the phone carriers have to approve business texting before texts to your customers start, usually 2 to 3 weeks. Your own texts with Glim (the 7:00 text, approvals, PAUSE) work from day one.

FAQ

What buyers ask first.

Do I need an office manager?

No. With one, Glim takes the chasing off their plate so they can run the office. Without one, it covers the phones and the follow-ups, and Full Assistant also answers your overflow line live. Same plans and prices either way. What it doesn't do: the parts of office work that need a person, like ordering parts or walking a client through a site.

Who writes the texts my customers get?

Every message is a written template we set up together, in your company's name, and you see each one before it's switched on. The AI may tidy the wording but can't add or change a number; if it fails, the plain template goes out. It never makes up a price or an amount.

Does it work with Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan or Square?

Not yet. Today Glim works from QuickBooks Online or a spreadsheet import (your office's sheet, Google Sheets or Excel). Use one of the others? Ask me; it isn't connected yet, and I'll tell you straight what works.

What happens if it breaks?

The parts that send texts are plain scheduled code on a cloud server, not my laptop. A failed text retries, and if it still fails the alert comes to me, never to your customer. You can text PAUSE any time. More in What happens if it breaks.
