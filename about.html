ABOUT SH. INDUSTRIES

Small software with honest limits and zero bloat.

sh. industries is a Massachusetts sole proprietorship started in August 2025 by Steven Hainley while studying Cybersecurity (B.S., Law & Technology minor) at Worcester Polytechnic Institute.

Why it exists

Most student software dies in a class folder or turns into a pitch deck with no working code. sh. industries exists to ship real, useful apps and security tools—tested on real hardware, priced fairly with one-time Stripe checkout, and kept private on your own device.

Four operating rules

  • Private on your device: Your shift history, gate codes, and security logs stay local on your phone or Mac—never sold to advertisers or brokers.
  • Plain English limits: Every hardware test and product boundary is published openly on the product page.
  • Fair pricing through Stripe: No $120/year subscription traps for simple tools, plus a 30-day money-back guarantee.
  • Direct builder support: One developer reads and answers support emails directly.
