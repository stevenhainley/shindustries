About sh. industries

Software that does the boring part.

sh. industries is a small software company in Massachusetts, started by a student. We build tools that handle the parts of running a business nobody has time for: the missed call, the follow-up, the reminder. Plain words, honest numbers, and you keep the final say.

The story

Started at a desk between classes. Built for people who work with their hands.

sh. industries began in 2026 as one student's company: Steven Hainley, studying cybersecurity at WPI, building the things he kept wishing existed. The first product is Glim, a second brain for a small business's phone. The idea came from a simple pattern: a plumber under a sink, a stylist mid-cut, a front desk with a line out the door, and a phone that rings out. That caller usually dials the next number. Glim texts them back in the business's own words before they do.

We are small on purpose. One founder, a lot of modern tooling, and a rule that every message a customer sees reads like a person wrote it. There is no sales team and no “call for pricing”: the price is on the page, the beta is called a beta, and the first ten businesses get a founding price kept for as long as they stay.

sh. industries makes two apps. Glim is the paid one, in a founding beta with a waitlist. GoodDucky, a free Mac app that spots USB gadgets typing like a machine, grew out of the cybersecurity coursework and is coming soon. More apps are on the way.

How we work

Four rules we hold ourselves to.

Plain words

Every screen and every text reads like a person wrote it. No jargon, no exclamation marks in your name, no price you didn't set.

Honest numbers

We never publish a number we didn't get. No invented customers, results or testimonials. The cost calculator on the Glim page uses your numbers, and says so.

You decide

Nothing goes out in your business's name without your tap unless you choose otherwise, and you can change that any time. Cancel from Settings, no call needed.

Your data is yours

Your customer list, quotes and message history can be exported or deleted whenever you want. We don't sell or share anything.
