Refunds

Last updated: October 26, 2026 (draft)

Try Glim for 7 days. If it is not working for you, you get all your money back.

The short version
  • Email glim@shindustries.co within 7 days of your first payment.
  • You get a full refund of your first payment ($29 on the founding beta price, $39 on the standard price). No forms, no hoops.
  • After that, Glim is month to month. Cancel any time in the app and you will not be billed again.

The 7-day money-back guarantee

The 7 days start the day you pay. If Glim is not right for you in that time, email glim@shindustries.co and ask for a refund. You do not have to explain, but we would like to hear what did not work.

We refund the full amount to the card you paid with, through Stripe. Your bank decides how fast it shows up, which is usually a few business days. [CONFIRM: confirm typical timing]

During the beta

Glim is in beta. The money-back guarantee is the same during the beta. If you are on the founding $29 price and cancel, you can come back later, but at the standard price. [CONFIRM: founding price on re-subscribe]

After the first 7 days

Payments after the first 7 days are not refunded, but you can cancel at any time so you are never charged for a month you do not want. If we made a billing mistake, such as charging you twice, email us and we will fix it. [CONFIRM: decide whether to refund a renewal charge requested within a few days, and say so here]

How to cancel

  1. Open Glim on your phone.
  2. Go to Settings and choose Cancel subscription. [CONFIRM: exact menu name in the app]
  3. That is it. No call, no email needed.

Cancelling stops the next bill. [CONFIRM: confirm whether access continues to the end of the paid month]

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and your data inside the app any time, whether or not you ask for a refund. See Privacy.

Texting numbers

If you cancel, your Glim number stops sending texts. [CONFIRM: what happens to the Glim number, and whether a business can keep or port it]
