How sh. Studio works

One link in. Eight finished clips out.

Founding pack: not worth $99 to you? Reply within 7 days of delivery for a full refund. Any pack more than 24 hours late is free.

  1. 01

    Send one episode

    A YouTube link or a file, your brand colour, anything that must stay out.

  2. 02

    Moments found and scored

    Glim reads a word-level transcript of the whole episode and scores each candidate moment.

  3. 03

    Steven reviews

    I cut, check and fix every clip: speaker crop, captions in your colour, −14 LUFS, under 9 MB.

  4. 04

    8 clips in 48 hours

    A private folder with the clips and a notes file of hooks and captions. One round of changes.

FAQ

What buyers ask first.

Is there a contract?

No. The Episode Pack is one payment per episode. Studio Monthly pauses or cancels any time.

What about refunds?

Studio founding pack: a full refund within 7 days of delivery if it isn't worth $99 to you; any pack more than 24 hours late is free.

What happens to my files?

Your episode and clips go to a private folder you own. Nothing is posted anywhere for you.
