Text messaging terms

Last updated: October 26, 2026 (draft)

Program name: Glim. This page explains the texts you might get from a local business that uses Glim, and how to stop them.

The short version
  • A local business (for example a salon, a clinic front desk, a cleaner or a plumber) uses Glim to text you after you contacted it.
  • Reply STOP to stop texts. Reply HELP for help.
  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • We never share your phone number or text consent with anyone for marketing.

Who is texting you

Glim is software made by sh. industries (Massachusetts, USA). A business that uses Glim sends texts to its own customers through Glim, from a Glim phone number. Each text names the business it is from. sh. industries does not send its own marketing texts to your customers.

What kinds of texts

Glim texts are about your service request, not ads. They include:

  • A reply when you called the business and nobody picked up.
  • Quotes, and check-ins about a quote you received.
  • Appointment reminders and confirmations.
  • Invoice and payment reminders.
  • A request to leave a review after a visit or a finished job.

Examples:

Hi, this is [Business], sorry we missed your call. What can we help with? Reply STOP to opt out.

[Business]: Your quote for [service] is $[amount]. Questions? Reply here or call [phone]. Reply STOP to opt out.

[Business]: Reminder, you're booked for [day] at [time]. Reply C to confirm. STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

Thanks for choosing [Business], [Name]! A quick review helps us: [link]. Reply STOP to opt out.

How you opt in

You get texts from a business only if you reached out to it first or agreed to hear from it. That means you did at least one of these:

  • Called the business or sent it a text.
  • Booked an appointment or a job, or asked for a quote.
  • Ticked an unchecked box on the business's booking or contact form.

If a business's form asks for your OK, the wording is:

I agree to receive texts from [Business] about my service request. Frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help. See Terms and Privacy.

Agreeing to texts is never required to buy anything or to get service from the business.

How to stop texts

Reply STOP to any text. You will get one reply confirming:

You are unsubscribed from [Business] texts. No more messages will be sent.

After that, Glim blocks that business from texting your number. You can also say STOP, CANCEL, END, QUIT or UNSUBSCRIBE. If you change your mind, text the business or call it and ask to start again.

How to get help

Reply HELP to any text. You will get:

[Business] via Glim. Help: glim@shindustries.co or [phone]. Reply STOP to opt out. Msg and data rates may apply.

Or email glim@shindustries.co. [CONFIRM: add a support phone number if one will be used for registration]

Cost and how often

  • How often you get texts varies. It depends on what you asked the business for.
  • Message and data rates may apply, based on your phone plan. The business and Glim do not charge you to receive texts.

Your phone number is private

We do not sell your phone number. Mobile information, including your number and your text consent, is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We share it only with companies that help us deliver the texts, such as our texting provider, and only for that purpose. More in our Privacy page.

Carriers

Wireless carriers and the companies that connect to them are not responsible for delayed or undelivered messages. Not all carriers are supported.

Businesses that use Glim

A business that uses Glim must follow our Terms, including only texting people who contacted it or agreed to hear from it, and honoring STOP right away. Glim blocks texts to numbers that replied STOP.
